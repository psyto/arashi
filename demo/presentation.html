The Fatal Flaw in Vol Strategies

"Short Perp = Vol Selling" — This assumption breaks in bear panics where vol is high but funding is negative. Shorts PAY instead of earn.
Fixed ±5% delta threshold — Allowing 5% directional exposure during high vol is a gamble, not a hedge.
"Extreme = 0%" is an evasion — By the time vol hits 75%, prices have already gapped. The exit comes too late.

Arashi's Multi-Layer Defense

Layer 1: Funding Polarity Gate — Hard block on entry when funding < 0. The PRIMARY condition, not volatility.
Layer 2: Regime-Adaptive Sizing — 5% in low vol → 35% in normal → 0% in extreme. Pre-extreme wind-down at 60%.
Layer 3: Dynamic Delta Thresholds — ±5% in calm → ±0.5% in extreme. Tightens when it matters most.
Layer 4: 30-Second Health Monitor — Last line of defense for jump risk and flash crashes.

Volatility Engine

YANG-ZHANG ESTIMATOR
σ²_YZ = σ²_overnight + k·σ²_OC + (1-k)·σ²_RS

Most efficient estimator. Handles drift + overnight gaps.
Weakness: struggles with discontinuous jumps
PARKINSON ESTIMATOR
σ² = 1/(4n·ln2) · Σ(ln(H/L))²

5× more efficient than close-to-close per observation.
Weakness: assumes no drift
REGIME CLASSIFICATION
Very Low (<20%) → 5% sizing, ±5% delta
Low (20-35%) → 20% sizing, ±3% delta
Normal (35-50%) → 35% sizing, ±2% delta
High (50-75%) → 15% sizing, ±1% delta
Pre-Extreme (>60%) → 7.5% sizing
Extreme (>75%) → 0% — full stop

Honest About Limitations

Jump risk: Yang-Zhang assumes continuous prices. Flash crashes create 1-2 cycle lag (3-6 min). The 30s health monitor is the backup.
Vol→funding correlation: Breaks in bear panics. The funding gate handles this, but Arashi sits idle — accepting missed opportunity over catastrophic loss.
Solana congestion: During extreme events, keeper transactions may delay. Max 1.5x leverage provides a buffer to liquidation.
We don't hide our weaknesses — we design around them.
