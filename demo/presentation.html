The Fatal Flaw in Vol Strategies

"Short Perp = Vol Selling" — Breaks in bear panics where vol is high but funding is negative. Shorts PAY instead of earn.
Fixed ±5% delta threshold — Allowing 5% directional exposure during high vol is a gamble, not a hedge.
"Extreme = close all" comes too late — By the time vol hits 75%, prices have already gapped. Need pre-emptive wind-down.

Honest About Limitations

Jump risk: Yang-Zhang assumes continuous prices. Flash crashes create 1-2 cycle lag (3-6 min). The 30s health monitor is the backup.
Vol→funding correlation: Breaks in bear panics. Funding gate handles this, but we sit idle — accepting missed opportunity over loss.
Philosophy: Capital preservation first. Alpha second.
